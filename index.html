      About the numbers

      Recipe ratios come from the official Transport Fever 3 wiki (industries and cargos page). Processor recipes are exact input→output ratios per production cycle. Raw industries output a plot-dependent range per year; the line rates you see are what you must ship to feed the chain at your chosen demand. Coupled byproducts (e.g. Steel Mill's steel, Livestock Farm's wool) are netted against demand in the chain and shown as surplus when nobody consumes them.

      Primary lines carry raw materials, secondary lines carry intermediate products between factories, and third lines deliver the end product to the town.

      Data: official TF3 wiki · offline page, no server needed
